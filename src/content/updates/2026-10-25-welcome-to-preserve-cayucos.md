Preserve Cayucos now has a home online. This is where we'll post news, meeting dates, and the research we gather on the issues facing our town.

A few things worth knowing:

**We're fiscally sponsored by Ecologistics.** That means donations go through an established nonprofit partner, with the oversight that comes with it. You can [give here](/donate/).

**We'll keep you informed.** Sign up for our email list below and you'll hear about upcoming events, public comment deadlines, and new reporting as it comes out.

**We want to hear from you.** If you have a concern about a project, a question about a county decision, or a skill you'd like to offer, [get in touch](/contact/).

Thanks for caring about Cayucos.
